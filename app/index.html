    The Field Guide

    Do it for real

    The whole thing in five steps, no game. Every real action happens in your own Zodl app.

    1. Install Zodl and write your 24 words on paper.

      Zodl (formerly Zashi) is a free Zcash wallet for iPhone and Android. Create a new wallet and copy the 24-word recovery phrase onto paper. Never screenshot it. Never share it. Nobody legit will ever ask for it.

      Get Zodl

    2. Get ZEC.

      Swap inside Zodl (it lands shielded), or withdraw from an exchange to your transparent address. Exchange deposits are public. Some exchanges, like Gemini, can send straight to your shielded address.

      Swapping into ZEC · Depositing from exchanges

    3. Shield.

      When Zodl shows a transparent balance, tap Shield. Zodl makes you shield before you spend, so your spending stays private.

    4. Send and receive.

      To send, paste a friend's address (it starts with u1), add a memo, and send. To receive, share your own u1 address from the Receive screen.

    5. Unshield only when you need to.

      Sending to an exchange or another transparent address makes that leg public: where it went, and how much. Avoid sending out the exact amount you just shielded.

    This site never asks for your 24 words, never stores your address, and has no tracking. It only teaches.
